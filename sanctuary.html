Are you ready
to truly rest?

A simple question, asked only once. The body knows the answer before the mind catches up.

Most of us have forgotten what real rest feels like — not the kind you collapse into after the day, but the kind that resets the breath, softens the held places, and leaves you slightly more yourself than when you came in.

Our Philosophy

Longevity isn't built
in the gym alone.

Real longevity rests on three pillars — training, nutrition and recovery. Most people invest in the first two and quietly neglect the third. It is the one that matters most.

Today, our deepest health problem is not effort. It's a nervous system that no longer finds rest. Chronic stress keeps the body in a constant state of alert. Sleep doesn't reset us. Days off don't feel restorative. Training, instead of building, slowly breaks us down.

Tulua is leading in Switzerland in uniting precision training with the science of true recovery. Strength built in the gym only becomes real when the nervous system can finally exhale.

The Practice

Somatic Bodywork
& Breathwork.

The door closes. The light dims. The room has been prepared for you. And for the next hour, there is only your breath, your body, and the slow undoing of everything you have been carrying.

Rooted in Traditional Thai healing arts, refined over years of one-to-one practice. Not symptom-driven, but state-driven — touching the breath, the fascia, the held places, until the nervous system understands it is finally safe to let go.

The body remembers what the mind has long forgotten. The work is to listen.

i
Traditional Thai Bodywork
The foundation. Rhythmic compression and assisted stretching, refined over centuries — performed clothed, on a futon, by a senior therapist.
ii
Fascia Release
The connective tissue beneath the muscle, freed of the tension it has been holding — sometimes for years.
iii
Deep Stretching & Mobilization
Joints opened. Range restored. The body remembering its full reach.
iv
Abdominal Tension Release
The most overlooked seat of held stress in the modern body. Worked slowly, with intention.
v
Guided Breathwork
The fastest route into the parasympathetic state. A practice the body re-learns to access on its own.
vi
Trigger Point Therapy
The single point that, released, allows three others to soften.
vii
Stress Regulation
Tools that go with you — off the table and into the week.
viii
Body Awareness
What you didn't know you were carrying. Felt, named, set down.

The Journeys

Three ways in.

One practice. Three different starting points — depending on what you bring through the door. Most members begin with one and, over time, find themselves naturally moving between them.

01

Stress
Reset

For when the nervous system can't find rest — the racing thoughts, the held shoulders, the sleep that doesn't quite work. Direct, deep relief through breath, fascia release and parasympathetic activation. The session most members begin with.

60–75 min
By appointment
02

Deep
Release
